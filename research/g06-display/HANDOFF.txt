additional size/reserved/u64-pointer envelope built by libmi3 ioctl_wrapper

/dev/mik!sys. The earlier two-level syscall interpretation was incomplete.

5. mik's built-in MI_DEV_SYS_ModuleInit can perform real display/OSD init:
   MI_DISP_INIT=1 and MI_OSD_INIT=1 in a COPIED mik_init.ini. Stock sets both 0.
   MI_DISP_Init call .text+0x34674; MI_OSD_Init .text+0x34a80.
   Success records MI_SYS_SetAttr(5,{selector=6 or 9,ready=1}).
6. fb open gate is MI_SYS_GetAttr(5,{selector=1,ready byte at +4}), NOT selector
   40 (mik loaded). Stock midaemon publishes selector 1 at 0x30e0 after init.
   A replacement must complete real init before publishing readiness.

   they are not in the link closure, but runtime registration may matter.
