G13 V013 raw read offset width, checked 2026-10-31

The installed HAL's a_mtktvfapi_sys_read_file takes an unsigned long long
offset. The caller and both wrappers preserve the 64-bit value in ARM r2/r3;
the seek origin is the next stack argument. The final call is fseeko64.

(saved under assets/research/new-tv-20261031T183329Z/artifacts/):

  0x25d92 loads the two offset words from the incoming stack.
  0x25dfe / 0x25e00 move them to r2/r3.

PLT mapping for these ELF files is .plt address + 32 + relocation_index * 16.
It was checked against the actual ARM instruction sequence and GOT relocation
addresses. The preliminary 12-byte-entry formula printed during exploration
was wrong and must not be used. The saved native disassembly is evidence of
instructions, not a substitute for checking relocation names.

This resolves the specific concern that ARM32 libc fseek/off_t might truncate
offsets above 2 GiB. It does not establish atomicity of an online capture.
Seek errors, HAL failures and short reads are also rejected by the helper.
