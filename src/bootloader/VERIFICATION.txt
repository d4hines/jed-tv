Bootloader verification map
==========================

Scope
-----
These are local source changes, not an installed TV image. The source follows
tvinit's split between decisions, an effect interpreter and physical adapters.
Use small state machines where the order of effects is the property being
checked. Keep bounds, field decoding and address arithmetic as direct functions.
Do not turn each register access into a state machine.

Ordering: Flow.lean and Flow/Proofs.lean
--------------------------------------
The actual authentication, network execution, update installation and journal
interpreters call Flow.run. Its transition table is compared with a separate
ordered program specification. It accepts only successful completion of the
current stage. Rejection and completion are terminal. A decreasing UInt32
budget makes this runner total; every successful program fits that budget.

Proved properties include:
  execution_requires_signature / execution_requires_both_digests /
  execution_requires_preparation:
    Reaching invocation requires successful signature, source-image digest,
    copied-image digest and CPU-preparation observations.
  image_write_requires_durable_invalidation:
    Image writing requires successful inactive-header invalidation and flush.
  header_commit_requires_readback_and_flush:
    Publishing a bank header requires signature success, a matching readback
    digest and successful flush. Earlier stages are prerequisites as well.
  journal_success_requires_readback:
    Journal completion requires successful record readback verification.
  reached_requires:
    The prerequisite property holds for arbitrary completion-event lists,
    including repeated, failed and out-of-order events.
  successful_runner_follows_program:
    The real monadic runner issues exactly the specified stages under the
    recording interpreter. Terminal/exhausted runners issue no effects.

tools/bootloader/FlowModel.lean additionally injects failure at every effect
in the four real workflows and checks the exact emitted prefix. The platform
callback implementations, truth of observations, and refinement from all
physical executions to completion traces remain obligations. A stage called
signature is not by itself proof of cryptographic verification.

Reception: Update/Receiver/Plan.lean and Update/Receiver/Proofs.lean
----------------------------------------------------------------
The production receiver decodes fields, calls the pure step function and
interprets its Decision. SessionId, ByteOffset and ByteCount distinguish
protocol roles. The phase is idle, receiving or sealed.

  sealed_never_appends / retransmission_never_appends:
    Sealed data cannot be extended. Old chunks request byte comparison,
    never overwrite received bytes. An explicit Abort permits a new session.
  seal_requires_complete:
    Seal requires the received count to equal the advertised total.
  wrong_session_cannot_write / rejection_preserves_state:
    A foreign session cannot change an active transfer's state, and rejected
    decisions preserve state. Session IDs are not authentication credentials.
  append_cannot_exceed_total / append_updates_exact_bytes:
    Accepted appends stay within the total and advance by the exact byte
    count, with no UInt32 wrap.

Parsing and bounds
------------------
Packet, manifest, boot-image, FDT, bootconfig, journal and LZ4 parsing remain
safe Lean over Reader/Writer capabilities. No parser imports a physical
implementation. audit.py checks local imports transitively, rejects direct
physical effects in the core and rejects foreign/unsafe definitions outside
the reviewed boundary. It is a source regression guard, not a compiler proof.
Ethernet/Descriptor.lean now owns the pure RX descriptor address/length
validation; Unsafe/Emac only obtains the words and handles DMA ownership.

Update/ParserProofs.lean proves that short/wrapping manifest spans and invalid
packet lengths reject before reading. Header checks reject invalid magic or
format before acceptance; those equations assume the stated pure read results.
The descriptor parser rejects unknown slots and only passes payload lengths
of at most 1514 bytes after removing the four FCS bytes. Proofs.lean contains
the underlying no-wrap/range and out-of-bounds-no-effect theorems.

This is not a proof of complete correctness of every parser. Memory ownership,
stable input bytes, DMA exclusion and register meanings are physical-adapter
preconditions. Some existing parsing loops are partial Lean definitions;
their totality and worst-case resource usage are not proved here.

Policy and executable addresses
-------------------------------
Proofs.lean covers the boot fallback cycle, journal selection/target and
checked memory arithmetic. Update/Proofs.lean covers authenticated bank
selection, compatibility, strict version increase and the other-bank target.
NetworkBoot/Proofs.lean covers aligned in-bounds entry instructions, address
arithmetic, busy-controller noninterference and rejected crypto-input spans.
NetworkBoot.Executable carries the entry-fit proof into the call adapter.

Physical update policy and rejection
-----------------------------------
Update/FlashPlan.lean fixes two application banks within upgrade. The
sector_within_banks and sector_excludes_journal proofs cover every allowed
bank/sector value, including the last sector; invalid_sector_never_invokes
shows rejection without calling the physical-address continuation.
sector_exact and different_banks_disjoint strengthen this to exact arithmetic
without wrap and prove that different banks never alias, at any valid offsets.

Update/FlashProofs.lean also checks the actual installManifest program:
rejected_signature_only_rejects proves it equals a pure error when the
verifier action equals pure false, for arbitrary Writer and Storage instances.
This includes insufficient workspace. It is an effect-program equality after
header parsing, not only a theorem about a trace stage named signature.
rejected_payload_performs_no_writes proves the corresponding effect equality
for a valid signature followed by a rejected payload digest.
It still assumes the stated verifier observation; it does not prove HACL* or
the FFI's behavior. first_storage_write_requires_authentication and
commit_requires_authentication_and_readback connect the workflow to the
ordering theorems above.

The physical adapter distinguishes unreadable storage from invalid images.
Read faults abort installation instead of authorizing initial provisioning.
The new gate exposes Ethernet before normal-kernel checks and publishes its
locally authenticated application choice through the final FDT, including
on the MBOOT-input fallback path. The normal Linux mount and EROFS driver
are outside these Lean proofs. Provisioning and physical power-loss tests
are still pending; see tools/bootloader/update/FLASHING.txt.

Power-loss specification: Update/StorageModel.lean
-------------------------------------------------
The specification separates durable media from a pending sector write. Reads
of the model's durable state cannot observe a merely queued write; flush
finishes it. A power cut may replace the pending sector with any bytes, so
tears need not be prefixes or retain intact bits. Events use the production
sector guard and a fixed target bank for an installation attempt.
other_bank_survives and journal_survives quantify over arbitrary finite event
lists and every protected byte. flushed_survives_power_loss
proves stability after a completed abstract durability barrier.

The explicit physical assumption is sector isolation: an operation cannot
damage an unrelated sector. Card firmware/FTL power-loss behavior, truth of
the hardware flush observation and formal refinement from driver execution
to this specification remain unproved. Scheduled ARM tests complement that
specification; they do not discharge these physical assumptions.

Trust and checks
----------------
ProofAudit.lean checks theorem dependencies, including generated termination
and equality helpers in the new runtime modules. It allows only propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound: no sorry or native-evaluation assumptions.
Proofs are erased; no proof evaluator or allocator is added to the firmware.

Crypto results are observations of pinned HACL* code, not a Lean proof of
Ed25519/SHA-256. Storage flush truth, physical torn-write behavior, DMA/cache
coherence, CPU transitions, stack bounds and the compiler chain remain trusted
or separately tested. A valid signature does not make uploaded code safe.
See Unsafe/CONTRACTS.txt for physical preconditions and Update/README.txt for
the replaceable root of trust and limits of software version checks.

Local commands (no TV access):
  nix develop .#bootloader -c python3 tools/bootloader/prove.py
  nix develop .#bootloader -c python3 tools/bootloader/check-safety.py

ARM build/audit and real-crypto fault checks are documented in
tools/bootloader/update/README.txt. They exercise compiled Lean with emulated
hardware; finite fault injection does not prove all physical failures.

Offline controller/durability review
------------------------------------
The previous ARM candidate was reproduced accepting a wrong-partition app
read and returning success after a write error appeared during card busy.
App reads now check live EXT_CSD just like writes. The driver rechecks errors
after busy clears, and flush rejects reset/disabled/busy-job controllers.

The stronger proof audit passes 519 theorem declarations (including generated
helpers), with the same three standard logical axioms and no sorry. The
existing 78 ARM/FCIE cases and 59 scheduled hardware-model cases pass. Together
these include 36 prefix tears and six non-prefix bit tears during card busy.
The scheduled checks validate saved vendor-source hashes and partition bounds;
exercise delayed commands, delayed DMA visibility and delayed persistence;
reject all tested R1 errors and wrong card profiles; and check timeout/reset
isolation and flush guards. No additional assembly or FFI was introduced.

QEMU reaches Linux userspace and rejects a malformed boot header. Network
execution, the six compressed-gate/fallback cases, sender retry/deadline tests
and controller/type-boundary checks also pass on the rebuilt candidate.
Full logs and hashes are under assets/build/leanboot-network/review-*.log,
flash-hardware-checks.json and validation.json; proof output is under
assets/build/leanboot-proofs/review.log. No TV access occurred. Card timing,
real durability, exclusive upgrade reservation and on-TV mounts remain
hardware/deployment obligations.
